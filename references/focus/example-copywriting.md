# Focus: copywriting

A focus narrows a compile run to one subject, so a single lane of your knowledge
gets deep, well-linked pages instead of being averaged into general notes.

Run it with:

    python3 scripts/compile.py --focus example-copywriting

Copy this file, rename it, and rewrite the two lists below for your own subject.
The compiler reads everything under the two headings verbatim.

## Include

- Hook frameworks and patterns
- Persuasion and conversion principles: emotional triggers, social proof, urgency
- Ad copy structures: VSL, UGC, storytelling, problem-agitate-solve
- CTA patterns and objection handling
- Messaging frameworks: how to frame an offer, benefits versus features
- Copy testing methodology: what was tested, what won, and why
- Channel-specific writing patterns: email, DM, ads, landing pages
- Voice and tone principles the owner has developed or observed

## Skip

Anything that is not about copywriting: accounting, logistics, product specs,
operational systems, general life notes, unrelated strategy. A later general
compile run will pick those up.
