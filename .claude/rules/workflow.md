# Workflow Rules

## Planning

Enter plan mode before architecture or design work, so the strongest model does
the thinking and you get a review gate before anything is written.

## The Brain's own skills

- `/grill-me` to stress-test a plan or pull what is in your head into a doc
- `/roast` to validate a new idea before building it
- `/one-thing` to find the highest-leverage move
- `/audit` to score the Brain's health and find the biggest gap
- `/review` for an end-of-session review pass over what you just changed

## Invariants that override convenience

Read the hard invariants block at the top of `CLAUDE.md` before writing anything
into `sources/`, `journal/`, `daily/`, `log.md`, or `me/`. Those are not style
preferences. Breaking them silently corrupts the provenance chain that makes
the wiki trustworthy.
